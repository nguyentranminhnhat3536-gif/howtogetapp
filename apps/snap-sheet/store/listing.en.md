## Title
SnapSheet: Document Scanner

## Short description
Scan documents to PDF on your phone. Works offline, no account needed.

## Full description
Turn paper into clean PDFs in seconds. SnapSheet scans receipts, contracts, notes and ID cards with your camera, trims the edges, brightens the page and saves it as a PDF you can share.

Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning and text recognition run on the device, there is no account to create, and there are no ads.

What you can do
• Scan one page or many pages into a single PDF
• Scan both sides of an ID card
• Import photos from your gallery and combine them into a PDF
• Search your scans by name, rename and delete them
• Share a PDF or the page images with any app
• Copy text from a scan (Pro). Works with English and Vietnamese text

Free and Pro
Free: scan as many documents as you like, up to 5 pages each, and export up to 3 times a day. No logo is stamped on your pages.
Pro: unlimited exports, text recognition, and up to 30 pages per document. Choose monthly, yearly, or pay once for lifetime access.

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play. The lifetime plan is a single payment with no renewal.

Available in English and Vietnamese.
