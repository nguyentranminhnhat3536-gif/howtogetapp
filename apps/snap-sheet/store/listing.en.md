## Title
SnapSheet: Document Scanner

## Short description
Scan documents to PDF, sign, merge and copy text. Your files stay on your phone.

## Full description
Turn paper into clean PDFs in seconds. SnapSheet scans receipts, contracts, notes and ID cards with your camera, trims the edges, brightens the page and saves it as a PDF you can share.

Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning and text recognition run on the device, your scans are never uploaded, and there are no ads. An account is optional: every feature works without signing in.

Scan and import
• Scan one page or many pages into a single PDF
• Scan both sides of an ID card
• Import photos from your gallery, or open an existing PDF
• Scan QR codes and barcodes, then copy, share or open the link

Organize and edit
• Folders, search, sort by date or name
• Reorder, delete and add pages
• Select several documents to share, move or delete at once
• Compress a PDF before sending it, or print it

Pro tools
• Copy text from a scan (Latin-script languages such as English, Vietnamese, Spanish, French and German)
• Sign documents with your own signature
• Add a text watermark
• Merge several PDFs into one
• Up to 30 pages per document and unlimited exports

Free and Pro
Free: scan as many documents as you like, up to 5 pages each, and export up to 3 times a day. No logo is stamped on your pages.
Pro: choose monthly, yearly, or pay once for lifetime access. When a free trial is offered, the price and the date the first payment starts are shown before you confirm.

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions). The lifetime plan is a single payment with no renewal.

Available in English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Indonesian, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Hindi.
